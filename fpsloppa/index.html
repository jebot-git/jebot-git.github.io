Step into the arena

Fast movement, textured 3D weapons and a mix of classic deathmatch and team objectives. Play with mouse and keyboard or tracked VR controllers, bring a VRM avatar, and meet friends on a player-hosted match or dedicated server.

Choose your kind of fight

Chase frags in Deathmatch, hold the hill, capture the flag or push through Assault objectives. Bomb Defusal brings round-based team play, while ST opens up outdoor terrain, jetpacks and vehicles.

Thirteen modes also include Team Deathmatch, Instagib, Instafreeze, Freeze Tag, Chainsaw Circus, TF and TB. Host your own session, browse dedicated servers, or start an offline practice match.
