About this game

Your weekend plans just got simpler: fish, grill, relax, repeat.

Find your spot by a lake, river, or coast. Choose your tackle, cast with tracked controllers, and work the reel as fish run and your line comes under tension. Choose classic, fly, feeder or lure tackle. Record discoveries and personal bests in your Field Guide, and spend earned in-game rewards on new tackle.

Take a break at the waterside BBQ: turn the food, share a serving, and enjoy the view. Play solo or join friends on a player-hosted session or dedicated server.

Free to play, with no real-money purchases. Find friends through EOS lobbies or connect through direct IP/LAN hosting. An independent Linux dedicated server is also available.
